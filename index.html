Dingo

Dingo is a private, personal assistant. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

With its owner's permission, Dingo reads the owner's own Google data (Gmail and Google Drive) to help him keep track of his documents, payments and reminders.

  • The data is used only to assist the owner. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own systems.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions.
